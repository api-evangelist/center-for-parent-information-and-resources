Michigan Alliance for Families is the federally funded Parent Training and Information Center for the state of Michigan. Michigan Alliance for Families is a statewide resource to connect families of children with disabilities to resources to help improve their children’s education. We help facilitate parent involvement as a means of improving educational services and outcomes for students with disabilities.
